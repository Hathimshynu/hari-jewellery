# Hero film (optional)

Add `hero.mp4` (H.264) and optionally `hero.webm` (VP9/AV1) here to play a
short cinematic film behind the opening title. It is detected at build time;
without it, the opening frame is the 3D necklace.

- 8–15 seconds, seamless loop, no audio needed (it plays muted).
- 1920 × 1080, under ~4 MB. Keep the centre calm — the title sits there.
- It does not play for visitors with reduced-motion or data-saver enabled;
  the poster (`public/images/jewellery/hero-necklace-wide.webp`) shows instead.
